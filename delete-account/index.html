Delete Your Account

Don't Be Late! — Konita Games LLC

This page explains how to request deletion of a Don't Be Late! account and its associated data, and what happens to that data afterward. It is a short, practical companion to our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, which remain the governing documents.

How to Request Deletion

How you request deletion depends on your role:

Teachers, Parents, and School or Program admins

  • In the app, go to Settings > Data & Privacy and choose to delete your account, or
  • Email support@dontbelategame.com from the account's registered email address with your school/class name, and ask us to delete the account.

Students

Student accounts don't have an email address on file, so a Student can't submit a deletion request by email directly. Instead:

  • Ask your Teacher or Program to remove you from the class and request deletion on your behalf, or
  • Have a Parent or legal guardian contact us directly at support@dontbelategame.com.

We may need to confirm your identity, or your authority over a Student's account, before completing a request.

What Gets Deleted

  • Account information: name, email and password (for non-Student accounts), and login credentials.
  • Gameplay and learning information: questions answered, accuracy, progress, star ratings, coins, achievements, and streaks.
  • Class membership: the account is removed from any class or roster it belonged to.

What We Keep, and For How Long

Deleting a class removes it and its roster from a Teacher's dashboard, but does not by itself delete the Students' accounts or their gameplay history — those are deleted separately, as requested or as described below.

Otherwise, we keep information only for as long as needed to provide the Service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes, and then delete or de-identify it. Inactive accounts and classroom rosters with no activity for twenty-four (24) consecutive months may be permanently deleted from our active systems even without a request. We also keep a record of the type and date of any privacy request itself (not its content), as explained in our Privacy Policy.

How Long It Takes

Deletion requests are reviewed and completed by our team and may take several days. We'll let you know once it's done.
